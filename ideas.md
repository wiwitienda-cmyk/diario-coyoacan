<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Neo-Brutalist Newspaper**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Authenticity**: Expose the grid, use high-contrast borders, and avoid subtle shadows. It feels like a freshly printed zine.
  2. **Information Density**: Pack information efficiently but with clear hierarchy, mimicking a newspaper front page but interactive.
  3. **Tactile Digital**: Buttons feel "pressable" with hard edges and hover states that shift position, not just color.
  4. **Mobile-First Utility**: The layout is strictly vertical-first, optimizing for the infinite scroll of social media feeds.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: Off-white / Newsprint (#F0F0E8) - Easy on the eyes, evokes paper.
  - **Text/Borders**: Ink Black (#1A1A1A) - Maximum contrast for readability.
  - **Accents**: **Avellaneda Rust** (#C05621) - A deep, warm orange-brown representing coffee and roasted nuts, used sparingly for CTAs and highlights.
  - **Secondary**: **Coyoacán Blue** (#2B6CB0) - A nod to Frida's house, used for links and map markers.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **The "Fold"**: A strong horizontal divider separates the hero (headline + image) from the details (map + menu).
  - **Card-Based Grid**: Content is chunked into rectangular cards with thick black borders (2px-3px).
  - **Sticky Action Bar**: A bottom bar on mobile with "Navigate" and "Menu" buttons always visible.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Marquee Scrollers**: A scrolling text bar at the top with "HOY EN COYOACÁN: CAFÉ AVELLANEDA..."
  - **Halftone Images**: Photos treated with a subtle halftone dot effect to simulate newspaper print.
  - **Typewriter Stamps**: "ABIERTO AHORA" or "RECOMENDADO" badges that look like ink stamps.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Snap Scroll**: Sections snap into place to give a structured reading experience.
  - **Map Expansion**: The map starts as a static "cutout" and expands to full screen on tap.

  **Animation**:
  - **Hard Cuts**: No fades. Things appear instantly or slide in with a hard stop.
  - **Hover Glitch**: Slight displacement on hover to simulate printing errors.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headlines**: **Chomsky** or **UnifrakturMaguntia** (Blackletter) for the main logo/header to scream "Newspaper".
  - **Subheads**: **Oswald** (Condensed Sans) for punchy section titles.
  - **Body**: **Merriweather** or **Lora** (Serif) for high readability in long text.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Warm Minimalist Editorial**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Breathing Room**: Generous whitespace to let the photography and text shine.
  2. **Sensory Focus**: Design elements that evoke the smell and warmth of coffee (soft blurs, warm tones).
  3. **Storytelling Flow**: The page unfolds like a magazine feature, not just a list of facts.
  4. **Social Sharability**: Every section is framed to look like a perfect Instagram story screenshot.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: Cream / Latte (#FFFDF5) - Warm and inviting.
  - **Text**: Dark Roast Brown (#3E2723) - Softer than black, easier for long reading.
  - **Accents**: **Sage Green** (#8DA399) - Natural, organic feel, used for backgrounds of secondary sections.
  - **Highlights**: **Gold/Amber** (#D4AF37) - For stars, ratings, and "Best of" badges.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetrical Balance**: Images and text offset each other to create dynamic tension.
  - **Full-Bleed Hero**: The main image takes the full width, immersing the user immediately.
  - **Floating Elements**: Cards float with soft, large drop shadows (neumorphism influence but flatter).

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Circular Reveals**: Images appear through expanding circles (like a coffee cup stain).
  - **Handwritten Notes**: "Must try!" or arrows drawn in a script font over images.
  - **Parallax Scroll**: Subtle movement of background elements (coffee beans, leaves) as you scroll.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Smooth & Slow**: Transitions are slow and eased (0.5s+), creating a relaxed vibe.
  - **Image Zoom**: Photos slowly zoom in when in view.

  **Animation**:
  - **Fade Up**: Elements gently fade up and move up as they enter the viewport.
  - **Soft Blur**: Backgrounds blur when a modal (like the menu) opens.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headlines**: **Playfair Display** (Serif) - Elegant, editorial, high contrast.
  - **Body**: **Lato** or **Inter** (Sans) - Clean, modern, invisible.
  - **Accents**: **Caveat** (Handwriting) - For the personal touches.

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Digital Bauhaus / Coyoacán Modern**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Form Follows Function**: Every element has a clear purpose; no decoration for decoration's sake.
  2. **Geometric Precision**: Use of circles, triangles, and squares to frame content.
  3. **Vibrant Contrast**: Bold colors inspired by Mexican vernacular design but applied with Swiss grid discipline.
  4. **Interactive Utility**: The map is the hero; the content supports the location context.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: White (#FFFFFF) - Clinical canvas for colors to pop.
  - **Primary**: **Mexican Pink** (#E4007C) - Bold, energetic, unmistakably Mexican.
  - **Secondary**: **Cobalt Blue** (#0047BB) - Deep and grounding.
  - **Tertiary**: **Sunshine Yellow** (#FFD700) - For highlights and active states.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Modular Grid**: The screen is divided into a strict 2x2 or 1x2 grid on mobile.
  - **Split Screen**: On desktop, map on one side, content on the other. On mobile, toggleable.
  - **Overlay Cards**: Information slides up over the map background.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Geometric Masks**: Images cropped into circles or arch shapes.
  - **Bold Lines**: Thick, colored lines separating sections.
  - **Iconography**: Custom, geometric icons for "Coffee", "Wifi", "Food".

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Direct Manipulation**: Swipe to change photos, pinch to zoom map (obviously).
  - **Toggle States**: Clear "Day/Night" mode or "Map/List" toggles.

  **Animation**:
  - **Slide & Reveal**: Panels slide out from behind others.
  - **Color Shifts**: Buttons change color drastically on hover/tap.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headlines**: **Montserrat** (Geometric Sans) - Bold, architectural.
  - **Body**: **Roboto Mono** (Monospace) - Technical, precise, easy to scan data like prices and hours.
</idea>
